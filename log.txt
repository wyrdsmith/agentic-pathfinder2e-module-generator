The players must find a way to neutralize the instability—either by performing a complex ritual to harmonize the resonance or by physically dismantling and sealing the core. Success halts the growth of the corruption, silencing the distress signal and ensuring the subterranean secrets remains buried once more. Failure to manage the energy’s volatility allows the "bloom" to expand further, creating a permanent zone of instability beneath the peaks.
